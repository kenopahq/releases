Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the macOS application Kandil, published by KENOPA LTD (the “app,” “we,” “our”).

What we collect

Nothing. Kandil does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Network activity

Kandil does not make any network requests during normal operation. The app runs entirely on your Mac.

The directly-distributed version of Kandil (downloaded from our website) checks for updates over HTTPS via the Sparkle update framework. These checks do not include any personal information; they retrieve a static appcast file that lists available versions. The Mac App Store version of Kandil does not include this update mechanism — updates are delivered by the App Store itself.

Permissions

Kandil does not request access to your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, files, or any other sensitive system resource.

Kandil uses Apple’s standard IOPMAssertion API to prevent your Mac from sleeping while the app is active. This is a public, non-privileged API.

Local storage

Kandil stores your app preferences (such as default activation duration, battery threshold, and similar settings) locally on your Mac using macOS standard user defaults. This data never leaves your device.

Analytics, tracking, advertising

None. Kandil contains no analytics SDK, no third-party tracking code, no advertising, and no telemetry.

Third parties

Kandil does not share any information with third parties because it does not collect any.

Children

Kandil is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and post the revised policy at the same URL.
