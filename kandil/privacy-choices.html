Your Privacy Choices

Effective date: May 1, 2026

This page summarizes the privacy controls available to you when you use Kandil, the macOS application published by KENOPA LTD.

Data collection  None

Kandil does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is therefore no data to opt out of, no profile to delete, and no setting to change.

Tracking  None

Kandil contains no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking code. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Network activity  Update checks only

The directly-distributed version of Kandil checks for updates by downloading a static appcast file over HTTPS. No personal information is sent. The Mac App Store version delegates updates to the App Store itself and makes no direct network calls.

How to revoke local data

Kandil stores your in-app preferences locally on your Mac using macOS standard user defaults. To remove this data:

  • Quit Kandil.
  • Open Terminal and run: defaults delete com.kenopa.kandil
  • Or simply move Kandil.app to the Trash. macOS will leave the preferences file behind unless you explicitly delete it; the Terminal command above removes it.
